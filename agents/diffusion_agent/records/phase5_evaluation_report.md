## Primary versus contrast

General evidence stays substantively stable: historical any-function GenAI use is not current ITSM penetration; broad AI is not GenAI-only; capability and one attributed deployment do not establish the population's timing distribution. E8 remains the strongest direct application deployment signal in both. Differences in wording are not source changes.

The primary begins with workflow mapping/baseline measurement and approval-dependent bounded local learning. The contrast explicitly emphasizes documented data flow, reviewer ownership, stronger comparable evidence and lower tolerance for unsupported inference before expansion. Both can consider an approved bounded exercise and neither requires a forced opposite adoption decision or arbitrary calendar delay. Both keep the same six-month endpoint.

Remaining timing ambiguity: primary recommendation_management_implication says “A more conservative organization could require stronger comparable peer evidence and documented reviewer ownership before the same trial”. Contrast says “Both primary and contrast scenarios could defensibly begin with approved bounded learning” and puts stronger comparable evidence “before wider use”. These are not a hard contradiction: primary is a hypothetical 'could', and reviewer ownership is shared. Nevertheless, the primary's speculation about another context is unnecessary and can blur whether stronger peer evidence is a trial prerequisite or an expansion prerequisite. The actual contrast case states stronger comparable evidence before wider use. This is a minor contextual-precision defect, not a reason to fabricate opposing decisions or disqualify the whole analysis.

## Regression and remaining-weakness assessment

- Excessive abstention: no material regression observed. Both withhold unsupported stages/rates/readiness while still giving source-supported signals, five factors, plausible mechanisms, conditional action and triggers. Conservative thresholds for category classification remain a scope limit, not a blanket refusal.
- Loss of detail: no required characteristic, analytical level, key source distinction or authority boundary was lost. Component-specific measurement for summaries versus notes remains present.
- Formulaic timing: labels help inspection and obey R4, but similar long prerequisite lists remain. Context-specific controls prevent a wholly generic response; differentiation is procedural rather than measured delay.
- Unsupported claims: the original 'beyond initial innovators' claim and invented confidence-reduction histories are absent as assertions. Primary's key hypothesized inhibitor, 'inability to demonstrate acceptable documentation quality and net reviewer-adjusted benefit', is labeled hypothetical but mixes an unresolved evaluation condition with an underlying performance barrier. It should not be elevated into an observed cause. The clearer risk field mitigates this phrasing, but the conceptual boundary still merits attention.
- Observed-inhibitor language: contrast says no inhibitor is established as a 'measured cause'. Lack of causal measurement is not the only possible form of observational support. The provided packet still supplies no documented peer constraint, so no concrete omission is established here; avoid interpreting that wording as a universal requirement for causal proof.
- Evidence limits remain unchanged: selective public packet, historical general survey, broader-AI abstract, one-vendor capability documentation, adjacent prototype and undated unaudited single-customer report. No current representative application penetration, empirical peer category or measured diffusion-driver ranking is established.
