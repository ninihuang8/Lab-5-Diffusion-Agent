## E1 McKinsey: The state of AI: How organizations are rewiring to capture value

- URL: https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai-how-organizations-are-rewiring-to-capture-value
- Date / observation period: Published 2025-03-12; survey 2024-07-16 through 2024-07-31
- Evidence type: Original survey; historical broad GenAI use
- Population / adoption definition: 1,491 respondents in 101 nations; 42% report employers above $500m revenue; national GDP weighting. Respondents describe their organizations, not a census of enterprises.
- Observation supported: 71% of respondents reported regular organizational GenAI use in at least one function.
- Limits and forbidden inference: Historical self-report, not an October 2026 estimate. Any-function use does not establish internal ITSM documentation deployment. No representative peer-population denominator; do not assign a Rogers category from this percentage.

## E2 US Census Bureau: The Microstructure of AI Diffusion, CES-26-25

- URL: https://www.census.gov/library/working-papers/2026/adrm/CES-WP-26-25.html
- Date / observation period: Published April 2026; abstract reference period Nov 2025-Jan 2026
- Evidence type: Original official survey working-paper abstract; broad AI context
- Population / adoption definition: US firms covered by the BTOS AI supplement; nationally representative as described by authors. The abstract does not supply the full sample size or full sampling-frame exclusions.
- Observation supported: Abstract distinguishes firm adoption, business functions and employee tasks; reports 18% firm AI use and 32% employment-weighted use during its reference period.
- Limits and forbidden inference: AI includes more than GenAI; worker activity differs from formal deployment. Percentages and weights have different denominators. Abstract-only inspection; no full-method audit. Not a current ITSM rate or a comparable global denominator.
- Retrieval locator: Working paper page abstract; opened 2026-10-22.
